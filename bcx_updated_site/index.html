Designed as a technology-first platform, BCX aims to bring transparency, efficiency, and reliable price discovery to the coal ecosystem by enabling seamless interaction between buyers and sellers through a secure, screen-based trading platform.

As a wholly owned subsidiary of GNA Energy, BCX combines deep expertise in energy markets with advanced digital infrastructure to create a marketplace that is transparent, accessible, and built for scale.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted digital marketplace for coal, setting new standards in transparency, efficiency, and market integrity.
